CSuggestive 2 contradictions

  • —Open: dose-response above 3 g, long-term safety.

Receipt 02 of 03

No effect of multi-ingredient greens powder on stool microbiome composition

Patel et al. · Gut Microbes · 2024
Study typeRCT, double-blind
Samplen = 72 adults
Duration12 weeks
FundingNIH R01 · independent
In plain English

A team gave 72 healthy adults either a greens powder or a matched placebo for 12 weeks, and sequenced their stool at start and end. The two groups looked the same. No statistically significant change in alpha-diversity (a measure of how varied the gut bacteria are). Effect size near zero.

Quality flags
B · RCT Preregistered No COI Adequately powered
Why this matters

A well-run independent RCT showing no effect is stronger evidence than a small open-label study showing an effect. That's why the verdict is Mixed, not Supported.

Where the literature disagrees.

Three live disagreements. We surface them so you don't have to find out from a comment thread.

Disagreement 1 · Mouse-to-human translation

Austad & Hoffman (2024) argue mouse lifespan effects rarely scale; Kaeberlein (2023) argues mTOR mechanism is conserved.

2 reviewsOpen question
Disagreement 2 · Optimal dosing

Weekly 5 mg (Mannick) vs daily low-dose (Blagosklonny). RCT data favors weekly; mechanistic argument favors daily.

MixedRCT incoming
Disagreement 3 · Immune effects

Some studies show immune enhancement at low dose; others show suppression at standard transplant dose. Dose-dependent.
